## Context & problem

Setting up a football game means finding enough players, a team to play against and sometimes a coach. Benched puts that in one mobile app, together with competitions and perks for players and coaches.

## What I built

I worked on the frontend and the backend API. The app is built with .NET MAUI and Blazor.
